About ScentSymphony

Founded in Grasse and now in Mumbai, we collaborate with independent perfumers to bring you small-batch, high-concentration fragrances. Every scent is a story — composed with rare naturals and modern molecules.

We believe fragrance is personal, sustainable, and deeply emotional. Our refillable bottles and ethically sourced ingredients reflect our commitment to beauty without compromise.
